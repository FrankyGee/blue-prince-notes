#### Notes
* First Era is named **Ajera**
* Originally named **Orindia**
    * Black banner
    
* Second Era saw the land split between the 4th King's children:
    * Northern Realm to the youngest - **Arch Aries** - land of the "Northern Storms". Rune: Mountains
    * Central Realm to the eldest - **Orinda Aries** - fields and mountains, heartspring of the kingdom. Rune: Plaque
    * Southern Realm to the daughter - **Eraja** - "mystical realm of the ancestors, a land steeped in tradition, magic, and the poetry of the old tongue." Rune: Hourglass

* Third Era
    * Saw construction of a vast network of railways

* Fourth Era
    * King Desilets III "The King of the People" moved the capital from **Reddington** to **Oris**
    * The nobles of **Fenn** were discontent with this (Located in the West)

* Fifth Era
    * Rebellion by "Prince" Leerson of Fenn upon King Desilets III's death
    * The real prince Desilets IV "shared ... little of his (father's) virtues"
    * Resulted in a savage campaign that destroyed Fenn: The War of the Fifth Era. 22 years, 1 in 3 men dead including Leerson
    * The rebellion succeeded with support from neighbors **Arch Aries** & **Nuance**
    * Black flags replaced with **red banners**
    * Remaining royal family fled South

* Sixth Era - Current
    * **Fenn Aries**, **Arch Aries**, **Eraja** "inherited by **The Three**" - rulers?
    * Spruce & Birch common across all realms
